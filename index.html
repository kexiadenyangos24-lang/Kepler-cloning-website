About Kepler College

At Kepler College, students learn by applying knowledge, demonstrating their skills through real-world projects and case studies rooted in Rwandan and regional contexts Each course is pratical, designed to equip students with the in-demand skills employees want, preparing them to succeed both locally and globally. Regardless of learners' primary subject, Kepler College students are equipped with 21st-century, transferable skills that help them work across sectors.

History

Kepler established Kepler College to scale its impactful education programs that have transformed the lives of thousands and their families. We received accreditation from the Government of Rwanda in 2022.
